TC3D-386: the facade art direction, prototyped

OSM Buildings facades, branch tc3d-386 from feat/osm-facades a36d2fd, with the TC3D-376 prototype applied under this one. Headless Edge, Vite dev server (vite:headless), 1596×800 canvas, building quality high, weather effects and clouds off. Day stills at 12:00, night stills at 22:00 local. The spec is the art-direction guide, docs/plans/osm-facade-art-direction.md.

The question. Does the guide's look answer "really basic, and not beautiful / detailed enough" and "windows cut off at the bottom or top of the building"? Every section shows the TC3D-376 buildings from the TC3D-376 cameras in three lanes:

  • Today: what the app draws now (the TC3D-376 still).
  • TC3D-376 proposed: that mock's look for the building (the TC3D-376 still).
  • Art direction: new. A throwaway shader (prototype.patch beside this page, not code that will land) draws the guide's section 5 items 1-6 for every class, then each class's one-glance read from section 3, forced on the shot's one building as in TC3D-376.

What every Art-direction still carries: vertical fitting (a 0.45 m base band, a ground storey, whole storeys stretched to fit, a parapet of at least 1.2 m, so no window is cut by the roof line or the ground; 2.1), clustered, varied lit windows at night (2.6), grime and contact darkening at the foot (2.4), glass as an interior behind a Fresnel sky reflection with blinds (2.3), tint jitter per building (2.4), and frames, recess shadows and sills, with mullions, rain streaks and PTAC grilles only up close (2.2).

The wall top is approximate. The shader still reads the wall height from the packed 2 m bucket, so storeys are fitted under the bucket's lower bound with the guide's 1.2 m parapet, and the coping is drawn at the bucket's centre, up to 1 m off the true roof edge. The exact top from geometry is TC3D-388's work.

What is approximate, and guide values not hit

  • Face ends are not known (2.1, 2.5, section 5 item 8, TC3D-385). Windows, bays and doors still run along each face from an arbitrary origin, so a face end can show a partial bay at a corner or a part seam, the fire-station doors run round every face including narrow returns, and there are no corner piers, no crew wing at one end, no hangar door, no docks on one face only, no house porch light per face.
  • Short walls lose rows. With the bucket's lower bound, a 0.45 m base, the class's ground storey and a 1.2 m parapet, a two-storey school or office often fits only its ground storey; a wall under 4.2 m (most 3 m boxes) shows no windows at all, and a fire station under 6.25 m no doors (SFO Fire Station 1).
  • Darker walls are the guide's palette. Split-face block #A39A8C and brick read far darker than the TC3D-376 tones; the walls in these cameras also face away from the noon sun.
  • Glass targets are not measured (2.3: L* 45-62 at 1 km, 15-32 within 300 m): the cameras are 30-220 m away and no L* was read from the stills.
  • Tint jitter is drawn from a hash of the lookup's ground point, so it changes when a tile reloads at another level of detail, and the parts of one building jitter apart.
  • The far tint folds base band, grime and coping in as one constant (0.95); lit-window clustering lowers the near lit share below the far tint's, so a tier change at night is not yet mean-preserving. The far path is unchanged otherwise.
  • Left out: the skybridge's ribbed-panel variant (hash 2), the hangar's small windows on other faces, the tank's obstruction lamp, the hotel's brick-panel variant and core tower, the garage stair core, generic schools' near-dark night share, and the low-sun warm haze is only sketched.
  • Canopy undersides by day add a small emissive for the lit ground seen through and the soffit's bounce, a value the guide does not give.
  • No rule exists yet. Each lane forces its class on the shot's one element; the cameras are 30-220 m from each building, not the tower view; geometry is the tileset's (canopies and skybridges are solid boxes); at 22:00 the imagery stays bright.
  • Flat walls with no lookup. A tile without the base lookup draws the far tint (Orlo Apartments in these cameras), as today.
