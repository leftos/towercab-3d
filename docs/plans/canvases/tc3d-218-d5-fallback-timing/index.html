TC3D-218 D5: what an untyped airport building shows before its shape class arrives

OSM Buildings facades, feat/osm-facades at e479e3d. Headless Edge, Vite dev server (vite:headless), 1596×800 canvas, building quality high. Delays measured at KSFO and KOAK; frames shot at KSFO from the tower eye, heading 313.9, pitch −1.9, FOV 9, at 12:00 and 22:00 local.

The question. Step 3 types an untagged building inside the aerodrome boundary from its shape (set B: footprint ≥ 1,500 m² and ≤ 20 m → HANGAR; GARAGE from 4-8 levels only when tagged). The footprint area comes from getTileFeatureStats, which attachBuildingLookup builds in its postRender drain after the tile loads. Until then the style can only say GENERIC; once the stats exist, tileset.makeStyleDirty() (once per drain pass) restyles and the next frame draws the typed facade. What should the controller see in between?

  • A, swap on arrival: GENERIC from the first frame, then the typed facade the frame after the stats arrive.
  • B, plain until typed: no facade grammar (a plain wall) until the stats arrive, then the typed facade.
  • C, fade: GENERIC first, then a 0.5 s crossfade to the typed facade.

Read. A. While panning, the stats are built in the same frame the tile is first drawn for 106 of 112 tiles (KSFO and KOAK together), so in A or B the controller sees the stand-in for one frame (17 ms). At that length C makes the change more visible, not less: a 0.5 s animation instead of one frame. B is worse than A at both lengths, since before the stats exist no rule knows which untyped buildings will become hangars. Every GENERIC building inside the boundary must start plain: 83 at KSFO and 104 at KOAK in the tower view's tiles, where only 12 and 10 become HANGAR. The rest pop from plain back to GENERIC. Long waits (up to 664 ms) happen only during the airport switch, when the whole scene is still streaming in and the frames themselves stall.

A: Swap on arrival

Worst case: a visible pop on every newly loaded tile while panning. Measured, that is one frame (17 ms) of GENERIC for 106 of 112 panning tiles, and at most 61 ms. At the airport switch it lasts up to 664 ms, as the lit windows at night go dark.

Only buildings that set B types change: 12 of the 83 GENERIC buildings inside the KSFO boundary in the tower view's tiles, 10 of 104 at KOAK. The others stay GENERIC from their first frame.

B: Plain until typed

Worst case: a plain building for the delay, then a pop to its facade. Until the stats exist no rule knows which untyped buildings will become hangars, so all of them start plain: 83 at KSFO and 104 at KOAK in the tower view's tiles. 71 and 94 of those pop back to GENERIC.

The same timings as A: one frame (17 ms) while panning, up to 664 ms at the airport switch. At night the plain wall is dark, so the windows switch on when the stats arrive.

C: Fade

Worst case: it costs a style animation per tile. Each typed building in a newly built tile needs its old and new class and a fade clock for 0.5 s. The packed colour's red channel is full (7 classes × 4 hash states = code 27 of 27), so the old class needs a new channel or a per-tile uniform, and the style or shader runs on every frame of the fade. At the median it turns a one-frame change into a visible 0.5 s one.

The fade starts the frame after the stats arrive (+17 ms at the median) and is complete at +500 ms; in the worst case it starts at +662 ms.

What is approximate

  • The clips are assembled from rendered stills on a fixed 60 fps timeline at the measured timings; they are not a screen recording of the step 3 code, which does not exist yet. Each state is a real render. In the worst case the app draws only 1-3 slow frames in those 662 ms; the clip shows 40 smooth ones.
  • Plain (B) is the PLAIN class (the domestic-garage grammar: flat wall, no openings, its own palette entry for the building's hash), not the tiles without the facade shader. The wall's tone differs from GENERIC's for that reason.
  • The fade (C) is an image blend of the GENERIC and HANGAR renders. The shader cannot blend two classes today (see C's worst case); an in-shader fade would match the blend everywhere except where the two grammars light differently.
  • "Before the tile renders" hides only this building (feature.show = false). A real tile brings its neighbours in on the same frame.
  • Measured in a dev build (unbundled Vite, headless Edge with a GPU, one machine, about 16.7 ms frames). A slower device (an iPad, a phone) was not measured.
  • The building is at KSFO, not KOAK. Every set B candidate inside the KOAK boundary has the 3 m estimated height of an untagged building, so from the tower only its roof shows.
