TC3D-316: the resting-height step at liftoff and touchdown

vNAS reports height at a reference point that rests R = 2.75 m (9.0 ft) above the runway for an A320. Ground contact enters when the reported height is within 3 ft of R and leaves above R + 5 ft; in contact the aircraft is drawn on the runway. How should the drawn height leave and rejoin the runway? Every lane flies the same data on one clock: parked 0–3 s, takeoff roll 3–11 s (rotation 10–11.5 s), liftoff 11 s, climb at 1,500 ft/min to 100 ft by 15 s, then a cut to an approach at 700 ft/min from about 63 ft with a flare from 21.5 s, touchdown at 23 s and the rollout. Contact leaves at 11.2 s (true wheels pass 5 ft) and enters at 22.3 s (below 3 ft, mid-flare), so in every lane the wheels stay on the runway until then and catch up over 0.25 s.
