TC3D-376: facade looks for more OSM building types

OSM Buildings facades, branch tc3d-376 from feat/osm-facades 71a5534. Headless Edge, Vite dev server (vite:headless), 1596×800 canvas, building quality high, weather effects and clouds off. Day stills at 12:00, night stills at 22:00 local.

The question. Which look should each OSM building type get? One section per candidate from the phase-1 survey (ranks 1-10 by share of what the tower sees), then the three small rule additions. Each section shows two real buildings, one camera per building, the same in every lane, so the lanes differ only in the facade the shader draws.

  • Today: what the app draws now.
  • Forced class: an existing class forced on that one building by wrapping the tileset style's colour (the fire-station mock's method); nothing else changes.
  • Prototype: a new look drawn by a throwaway shader branch behind a new class id (CANOPY 7, RETAIL 8, WAREHOUSE 9, FIRE_STATION 10, HOTEL 11, TANK 12), forced the same way. The prototype is kept as prototype.patch beside this page and is not code that will land.

1. Canopy / open roof

building=roof (324, 109 of them solar with power=generator), building=canopy, amenity=shelter (47), parking=carports. 360 elements, 6.46% of the view. Today GENERIC (356): punched windows on a solid 3 m box.

Survey proposal: a new look, a fascia over a dark void with posts. 356 of 360 have no min_height, so the box stays solid from the ground.

  • KOAK solar roof at the tower's foot (way 468786430): today GENERIC; 465 m², 3 m box, about 100 m from the eye.
  • Costco Gasoline fuel canopy, KSJC (way 225007882): today GENERIC; 679 m², 3 m box.

Today (GENERIC)

GENERIC on both: punched windows on a solid 3 m box, nothing that reads as an open roof.

Proposed: CANOPY (prototype)

Prototype. A 0.75 m light fascia under the roof line, a near-black recessed void below it and 0.6 m posts every 7 m. It fakes an open roof on posts: the tileset still draws a solid box, so the roof top stays solid and nothing shows through the void; no light at night.

What is approximate

  • No rule exists yet. Each lane forces its class on the shot's one element from the page; a real rule would type every building with those tags.
  • The prototypes are sketches. Door, bay and window sizes, palettes and lit shares are first guesses to show the idea, not tuned values.
  • The cameras are not the tower view. They sit 30-220 m from each building so the facade can be read; from the tower eye most of these buildings are several hundred metres to 2 km away.
  • Geometry is the tileset's. Canopies and skybridges are solid boxes standing on the ground, and most untagged buildings are 3 m boxes whatever their real size; a facade can only paint the walls it is given.
  • Night ground. At 22:00 the buildings darken and their lights show, but the satellite imagery stays bright.
  • Weather is off (clouds, precipitation and weather effects): live METAR clouds drew across the walls.
  • Swapped examples. Where a survey example lay at KLAX or KHWD, a building of the same type at KOAK, KSJC or KSFO was shot instead (marked "swapped in" above).
